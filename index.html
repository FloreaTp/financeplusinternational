QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Questions before you get started?

Here are some useful points to consider before making a financing decision.

Ask a question →
Does the calculator show my final interest rate?

No. The rate is an illustrative input chosen for estimation. Your actual rate, fees and terms—if an offer is available—must be confirmed in writing by the lender.

Does submitting the form guarantee approval?

No. Submitting an enquiry is not a credit decision or a promise of funding. Eligibility and approval depend on a proper review and the applicable terms.

What documents might be requested?

Depending on the product and jurisdiction, a provider may request identity, residence, income or business information. Share sensitive documents only through a verified, secure channel after confirming who will receive them.

Are there fees beyond interest?

There may be fees or other costs. Review the full written offer, including the annual percentage rate where applicable, all charges, repayment schedule and consequences of late payment.

How can I check that a financing provider is legitimate?

Independently verify the legal company name, address, licensing or registration where required, and regulator records. Do not pay upfront fees to unlock a promised loan without independent verification.
